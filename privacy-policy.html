Privacy Policy — TingleBox Uploader

Last updated: 25 October 2026

What this app does

TingleBox Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels (TingleBox and Visual Crafts) through the YouTube Data API.

Google data used

The app requests a single Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload ("Manage your YouTube videos"). This permission is used only to upload video files that the owner chooses, to the owner's own channels. The app does not read, list, or download private channel data, and it does not access Gmail, Drive, or any other Google service.

Data collected and stored

When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives the standard OAuth tokens needed to perform uploads. Tokens are stored securely by the owner's automation service and are used only to carry out uploads the owner directs. Video files are processed only for the purpose of uploading them. No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools are used.

Data sharing

The app does not sell, share, or disclose Google user data to any third party. Data is never used for advertising or for training models.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from Google Account > Security > Third-party access. After revocation, the stored tokens stop working. To request deletion of any remaining data, contact the email below.
